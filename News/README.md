# आवाज (Aawaaz)
*Product of THE SONAR*

**आवाज** is a digital broadsheet featuring a **clean headings-only Pinterest masonry grid** on the homescreen with an authentic **soft newsprint paper aesthetic** in light mode and **pure deep black (`#000000`) high-contrast styling** in dark mode. The **आवाज** logo sits centered in the middle of the screen. Clicking any headline opens the **full-screen in-depth investigative article** on its own dedicated page.

---

## Design & Experience Highlights

1. **Focused In-Depth Journalism (Clean Single Flow)**:
   - Clicking any headline opens the dedicated full-screen page (`/article/<id>`).
   - Dissects each story into clear, investigative pillars:
     - **The Executive Verdict**
     - **What Transpired in the Past 24 Hours** (fact-checked breakdown)
     - **Investigative Dissection & Global Context**
     - **Macroeconomic & Technology Stakes**
     - **Original Wire Source Attribution**
   - Free of speculative sidebars or generic takeaways.

2. **Slim, Modern Footer**:
   - Short, unobtrusive, compact single-bar footer.
   - Prominently displays:
     - **`⚠️ AI can make mistakes, so double verify each news.`**
     - **`Product of THE SONAR`**
     - Hindi letter **"आ"** brand mark and **आवाज** logo.

3. **Centered Logo (Middle of the Screen)**:
   - The regal **आवाज** masthead and Hindi **"आ"** brand seal are centered in the middle of the screen.
   - Slogans and clutter have been removed for an iconic look.

4. **Soft Paper Look & Pure Dark Black High Contrast**:
   - **White/Light Mode**: Soft, warm newsprint paper tone (`#f5f0e6`) with subtle paper cards (`#fcf9f2`) and carbon charcoal ink typography.
   - **Dark Mode**: Completely dark pure black (`#000000`), pitch obsidian cards (`#080808`), crisp bright white headings (`#ffffff`), and sharp high-contrast reading text.

5. **Homescreen: Headings Only**:
   - The Pinterest masonry grid displays **only headlines** along with category badges and timestamps.
   - Direct link on each heading to open the full article.

6. **Reset Mandate**:
   - Displays **`Resets every 24 hours`** with a subtle live pulse.
